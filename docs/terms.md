# Terms of Service

**DAYSHOT** — last updated 1 September 2026

## What this is

DAYSHOT is a free game that runs inside Reddit. Using it means agreeing to these
terms, and to [Reddit's User Agreement](https://www.redditinc.com/policies/user-agreement)
and [Content Policy](https://www.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/205926439),
which apply to everything you do on Reddit including here.

## The game

One attempt per Reddit account per UTC day. That is the whole idea, and it is
enforced by the game: there is no retry, no second chance, and nothing that can
be bought. Scores are computed on the server from how long you held the button;
the score your browser shows you is a preview, and the server's answer is the
one that counts.

## Fair play

Do not try to submit results you did not play — automating input, replaying
another player's input, or tampering with what the game sends. Accounts that
appear to be doing so may be excluded from the leaderboards, silently and at our
discretion, and the game's operator may ask Reddit to act under Reddit's own
rules. Using multiple accounts to take multiple shots at the same day is
likewise not in the spirit of a game whose entire premise is one attempt.

## Your content

If you choose to post your score card as a comment, that comment is yours: it
appears under your username and you can edit or delete it like any other Reddit
comment. The game will never post, comment or subscribe on your behalf without
you pressing a button that says so and confirming what will be published.

available, correct, or preserved. Scores, streaks and history may be lost — for
example if the app is removed from a community, if Reddit's platform changes, or
through ordinary failure. Data tied to a specific day is deleted after 90 days
by design.
