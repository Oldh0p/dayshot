# Privacy Policy

**DAYSHOT** — last updated 1 September 2026

DAYSHOT is a game that runs entirely inside Reddit. It has no website, no
accounts of its own, no servers of its own, and no third parties. This policy is
short because there is not much to describe.

## What is collected

Only what the game needs to work:

- **Your Reddit account identifier.** The opaque `t2_…` id Reddit gives the app.
  The app never asks for, sees or stores your password, your email, or anything
  else about your account.
- **Your shot.** For each day you play: how long you held the button, the
  resulting score, and the time you submitted.
- **Your progress.** Your streak, your longest streak, your best score, how many
  days you have played, and whether you have completed the first-time warm-up.
- **Your username, for the leaderboard.** So other players can see who is on the
  day's board.
- **Whether you agreed to comment as yourself**, so the app asks once rather
  than every day.
- **Counts that belong to nobody.** How many shots were taken on a given day,
  how many were bullseyes, and so on. These are totals only and cannot be traced
  back to a person.

The game also keeps a couple of preferences in your browser's own storage — the
sound toggle and your practice tally for the day. Those never leave your device
and are not readable by anyone, including us.

## What is not collected

No email address, no location, no device fingerprint, no advertising identifier,
no browsing history, no contacts. Nothing is inferred about you. There is no
analytics provider, no tracking pixel, and no external request of any kind: the
app declares no network access outside Reddit, so data physically cannot leave.

## Where it is stored

In Reddit's own managed storage, provided to the app by the Developer Platform.
It is not copied anywhere else. The app operator has no separate database,
server or backup.

## How long it is kept

- Anything tied to a specific day — your shot, that day's scores, that day's
  usernames — is deleted automatically after **90 days**.
- Your streak and personal records are kept while you keep playing, because a
  streak that expired would defeat the purpose of a streak.

If you delete a score comment on Reddit, the app is told and forgets its record
of that comment.

## Who it is shared with

Nobody. It is never sold, licensed, rented, shared with data brokers or ad
networks, or used to train any model. There is no third party involved in
running this game.

## What is public

Your username and score appear on the day's leaderboard inside the game, visible
to other players. If you press the button that posts your score card as a
comment, that comment appears publicly on Reddit under your username — the app
shows you the exact text first, and you can delete the comment yourself at any
time, like any other comment.

## Your choices

- You can play without pressing anything that posts on your behalf. Nothing in
  the game is locked behind sharing or subscribing.
- You can play without an account at all: a visitor who is not logged in gets a
  practice shot on a fixed demo level.
- You can delete a score comment yourself, at any time, from Reddit.
- To have your stored game data removed, contact us (below) and we will delete
  it.

## Children

The game is not directed at anyone under 13, in line with
[Reddit's own terms](https://www.redditinc.com/policies/user-agreement).

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.

## Contact

Reach the developer through Reddit modmail at the game's home community, or by
opening an issue on the project repository.
